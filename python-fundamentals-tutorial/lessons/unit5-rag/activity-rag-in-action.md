# Activity: RAG in Action

## Objective
Experience the difference between a standard chatbot and a Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) chatbot.

## Instructions
1. Form pairs or small groups.
2. Each group will ask the same question to both a regular chatbot and a RAG-powered chatbot (use provided online demos or teacher's guidance).
3. Record both answers.
4. Discuss:
   - Which answer is more accurate?
   - Does the RAG answer cite sources?
   - How does the information differ?
5. Share your findings with the class.

## Example Questions
- What is the capital of France?
- How does photosynthesis work?
- Who invented the telephone?

## Reflection
Write a short paragraph about what you learned from this activity.
